A draft. Edit freely; keep the claims in line with `server/public/privacy.html`.

## Basics

- **Name:** Cabine
- **Summary** (in the manifest's `description`; up to 132 characters):
  Keep your wardrobe beside every online store, and see store pieces styled with clothes you already own.
- **Category:** Shopping
- **Language:** English

## Description

> Shopping online, you can't tell whether that jacket goes with your jeans until it arrives. Cabine puts your wardrobe right next to the store.
>
> **Take it to Cabine.** Right-click any product image on any store and choose "Take it to Cabine". It lands in your Fitting Room, already sorted as a top, bottom, dress, outerwear or shoes.
>
> **Your closet, beside every store.** Add photos of clothes you own from your computer, or from your phone with a QR code. Cabine cleans each photo into a neat product shot.
>
> **See them together.** Pick a store piece and a few of your own. Cabine dresses a mannequin in the whole look, so you can judge it before you buy.
>
> **Save the looks you like** and come back to them. Open the original product page in one tap.
>
> **Optional account.** Sign in with email or Google to keep My Closet and your Saved Looks on every computer. Without an account, everything stays in your browser.
>
> Privacy: photos are sent only to make a look or clean up a photo, and those results are deleted after 30 days. No ads, no trackers, and your data is never sold. Full policy: https://cabine-server.vercel.app/privacy
>
> Free to use, with a daily limit of looks and photo clean-ups.
